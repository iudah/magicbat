1. This is a nail;
That is a nail.
2. Look at that snail;
He has a short tail.
3. Put the snail in the big pail.
4. Tell Tom to bring me a nail.
5. When the stars at set of sun Watch you from on high, When the morning has begun Think the Lord is nigh.
6. Do all the good you can, In all the ways you can, Just as long as you can, To all the people you can.

This is lit-tle Har-ry Brown. He can-not walk ver-y well. He of-ten falls, but he does not cry. He puts out his hands, so as not to hurt his head.
Have you a ba-by at home? Yes, and he can talk and walk. He does not talk like me. He says fing for thing, and tat for cat.
Our ba-by has two ears, two eyes, a mouth, a nose, ten fin-gers and ten toes.

They stop by the bars And switch their tails, Till the girls bring out Their milk-ing pails.
